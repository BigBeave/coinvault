Overview

CoinVault is an iOS-optimized platform designed specifically for numismatists, coin roll hunters, and online resellers. It provides:

  • Live Camera & Hardware Shutter: Front (Obverse) and Back (Reverse) photo captures with circular coin reticle, alignment guides, native iOS hardware camera launch (bypasses iOS HTTP WebRTC restrictions), and photo library picker.
  • AI Coin Identification: Powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision to recognize coin series, denomination, year, mint mark (P, D, S, CC, O, W), strike type, and condition, with full offline catalog fallback.
  • Material Composition & Bullion Melt Calculator: Reports exact metal composition (e.g., 90% Silver, 24K Gold) and computes live bullion melt values based on spot prices, with live spot customization in Settings (⚙️).
  • High-Dollar Varieties & Key Dates: Highlights the specific dates, doubled dies (DDO/DDR), repunched mint marks, overdates, and rare errors that bring the most money (e.g. 1955 DDO Penny, 1937-D 3-Legged Buffalo, 1893-S Morgan, 1982 No-P Dime) with value estimates up to $100,000+.
  • 1-Click Multi-Platform Selling Listings: Instantly generates selling drafts across 4 major online marketplaces: eBay, Mercari, Whatnot, and Reddit r/CoinSales.
  • Listing Photos Showcase & Mobile Save: Displays your coin photos directly inside the selling tab with a 1-tap "Save Photos" button to save images to your iPhone camera roll.

How to Run CoinVault (3 Options)

Option 1: 100% Standalone iPhone Mode (No PC Needed)

CoinVault now bundles the entire numismatic database, melt calculations, key varieties, and selling engine directly in client-side JavaScript. It runs 100% inside iOS Safari / PWA without needing a local computer server at all!

  • Tap ⚙️ Settings → Set Operation Mode to ⚡ Standalone iPhone Mode.
  • (Optional) Enter your free Google Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com for direct mobile AI vision.
  • Tap Share in Safari → "Add to Home Screen" to install as a permanent native app.

Option 2: Free 1-Click Cloud Hosting (Public HTTPS URL)

Host CoinVault on a free public HTTPS web address accessible from anywhere on 5G or Wi-Fi with your PC turned off:

  • GitHub Pages: 100% free static PWA hosting with zero servers (pre-configured at root).
  • Render: 1-click deploy of the full Python backend using render.yaml.
  • Vercel: 1-click deploy using vercel.json.
  • See COINVAULT_STANDALONE_GUIDE.html for step-by-step instructions.

Option 3: Local PC Server Mode

Start the local server on your PC: python run_server.py

On your iPhone (same Wi-Fi), open Safari to: http://<YOUR_COMPUTER_IP>:8000

Features Walkthrough

1. Scanning & Taking Photos

  • Use the viewfinder with circular guide to frame the coin.
  • Tap the shutter button to take a photo or launch the native iPhone camera, or choose from your iPhone photo library.
  • Capture both the Front (Obverse) for date, and Back (Reverse) for mint mark.
  • Or test immediately using the 1-Tap Sample Coins carousel.

2. Numismatic & Market Valuation

  • Composition: Weight, diameter, fineness, and precious metal troy ounces.
  • Melt Value: Live calculation of intrinsic gold/silver bullion value.
  • Grade Breakdown: Market value estimates across Good (G-4), Fine (F-12), Extra Fine (EF-40), AU-58, and Mint State (MS-65+).
  • Top Money Varieties: Table of valuable varieties, errors, and key dates to watch out for.

3. Making Selling Listings

  • Open & Pre-fill on eBay: Direct link that opens eBay's listing creation page with the title and category pre-filled.
  • Copy HTML: 1-click copy of the responsive, professional listing description template.
  • Save Photos: 1-tap button to save listing images to camera roll.
  • Mercari / Whatnot / Reddit: Dedicated tabs for instant 1-click cross-posting.
  • Publish via API: Add your eBay User Token in Settings (⚙️) to push inventory drafts directly into your eBay Seller Hub.
