================================================================================
COINVAULT — iOS NUMISMATIC AI & MULTI-PLATFORM SELLING SUITE GUIDE
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
CoinVault is a mobile-first iOS platform (PWA) designed for coin collectors,
dealers, and online resellers. It allows you to:
1. Use your iPhone camera or camera roll to take and load photos of coins.
2. Accurately identify coin series, year, mint mark, composition, melt value,
   rarity, and market prices across grades.
3. Automatically highlight high-dollar varieties, key dates, and mint errors
   (e.g., 1955 Doubled Die, 1937-D 3-Legged Buffalo, 1893-S Morgan, 1982 No-P Dime)
   that bring the most money online.
4. Hit "Make Listing" to instantly generate selling listings with photos across
   multiple platforms:
   - eBay: SEO titles (<= 80 chars), categories, responsive HTML descriptions,
     pre-filled deep links, and eBay Inventory API draft payloads.
   - Mercari: Optimized title, pricing, coin specs, hashtags (#silver, #coins).
   - Whatnot: Fast livestream auction cards with starting bids and sudden death notes.
   - Reddit r/CoinSales & r/Pmsforsale: Formatted Markdown [WTS] sale posts.

RUNNING WITHOUT YOUR PC (STANDALONE & CLOUD MODES)
-------------------------------------------------
You can run CoinVault completely without your PC involved, powered on, or connected:

1. STANDALONE ON-DEVICE MODE:
   - CoinVault bundles the entire numismatic database, bullion melt formulas,
     high-dollar varieties, and listing engine directly in static/js/coin-engine.js.
   - It runs 100% inside iOS Safari / PWA even when your computer is shut down.
   - Connect your free Google Gemini API key in Settings (⚙️) to perform direct
     AI vision recognition from iPhone Safari to Google Cloud.

2. FREE 1-CLICK CLOUD DEPLOYMENT:
   - Render (Free Web Service): Uses render.yaml & requirements.txt for a live HTTPS URL.
   - GitHub Pages (100% Free Forever): Hosts the static client PWA with zero servers.
   - Vercel (1-Click): Uses vercel.json for instantaneous global CDN hosting.
   - Railway / Docker: Uses railway.json, Procfile, and Dockerfile.
   (See COINVAULT_STANDALONE_GUIDE.html or COINVAULT_STANDALONE_GUIDE.txt for full walkthroughs).

3. LOCAL PC WI-FI MODE (OPTIONAL):
   - python run_server.py
   - Navigate to http://<YOUR_COMPUTER_IP>:8000 on your iPhone Safari.

INSTALLING AS NATIVE iOS APP
----------------------------
1. In Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow).
2. Tap "Add to Home Screen" and tap "Add".
3. CoinVault is now installed with a native app icon, offline caching, and full-screen UI!

KEY FEATURES
------------
- Dual Photo Capture with Circular Reticle & Real-Time Front/Back Slots.
- Direct Photo Download: 1-tap "Save Photos" downloads photos to iPhone Camera Roll.
- Instant 1-Tap Demo: 10 pre-loaded sample coins (US, Ancients, World Bullion, Banknotes).
- 32-Series Database: US Classic, Ancient Roman & Greek, World Bullion, Banknotes.
- Material Composition & Bullion Calculator: Computes live melt values from spot prices.
- High-Value Dates & Varieties: Full database of rarest varieties with $100,000+ estimates.
- Multi-Platform 1-Click Listing Engine: eBay (with <= 80 char title counter and web prelist deep link), Mercari, Whatnot, and Reddit.
- Settings Modal (⚙️): Switch between Standalone and Server mode, customize spot prices, and save Gemini API key.
